What does this dress code mean?

Jacket required

Common at upscale restaurants, clubs and members' clubs: men wear a jacket. 'Jacket and tie' means a tie as well.

For men

Sport coat or blazer with a shirt and smart trousers; no jeans or sneakers. Tie only for 'jacket and tie'.

For women

No fixed rule; dress to a similar level: smart dress, or trousers or skirt with a blouse.
